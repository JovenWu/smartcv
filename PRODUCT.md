# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Recruiters screening a batch of CVs against a role they defined. They work at a
desk, in a browser tab that stays open while a batch processes. Their job:
define criteria, confirm weights, watch results stream in, inspect evidence,
and decide the shortlist. They are the decision-maker; the tool organizes
evidence.

## Product Purpose

SmartCV reduces manual CV-comparison effort by laying out per-criterion,
source-backed evidence in a transparent scorecard. Every excerpt links to a
page in the source document; every score is inspectable and correctable. The
product deliberately never rejects or ranks-on-trust alone: low-confidence and
contradictory results stay visible for review.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, honest. A tool that earns trust by showing its work rather than
by looking clever. Monotone discipline; nothing decorative.

## Anti-references

- Gamified or "AI magic" hiring tools with confident green/red verdicts and no
  visible evidence
- Dense enterprise-ATS dashboards with filter rails, badges everywhere, and
  five competing accent colors
- Marketing-style landing aesthetics inside a work tool (hero copy,
  illustrations, gradients)

## Design Principles

- Evidence first: every judgment links to verbatim source text with a page.
- Rank provisional until final: labels state when ordering may still change.
- Uncertainty stays visible: review flags never hide or sink a candidate.
- One ink, one paper: monochrome surfaces; status conveyed through shape,
  weight, and icon, not hue.
- Compact over sparse: the scorecard optimizes for scanning a full batch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast. Full keyboard access to evidence and review controls.
Reduced-motion friendly: no scroll animations; row updates appear without
motion. Status is never color-only (icon + text label always).
